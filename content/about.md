ScoutmasterCG.com was the online home of a long-running blog and podcast focused on practical leadership in Scouting. Over the course of nearly a decade, the site accumulated roughly 1,700 posts and more than 300 podcast episodes covering troop leadership, the patrol method, youth development, advancement, outdoor program planning, and the everyday realities of serving as a volunteer Scoutmaster. The material reflected the perspective of a working unit leader speaking directly to other adult volunteers, often in response to real questions from the field.

This archive preserves that body of work as it was originally created. The episodes and posts reflect the policies, products, links, and organizational structures in place at the time they were published; some references may now be outdated. Any current Scouting requirements, safety guidance, or program details should be confirmed with the appropriate official sources.

The author, Clarke Green, remains alive and well. However, the site is no longer an active project. There is no ongoing publication schedule, updating, or expansion planned. The purpose of this archive is preservation and reference, not continued development.
