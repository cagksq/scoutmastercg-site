Is Scouting a system of measurement?
Scouting is more, much, much, more, than a system of measurement, yet it has lots of “yardsticks”.  Baden-Powell wrote “
Scouting is a game. But sometimes I wonder whether, with all our pamphlets, rules, conferences, and training classes we may not appear to be making of it too serious a game. ”
How way we use the yardsticks of the Scouting program is all important to reaching our aims, but measurements and metrics are not our focus. There is a measurement that indicates we are succeeding, and I'll explain exactly what I mean in this podcast.
Bobwhite Blather
by Frank Maynard
