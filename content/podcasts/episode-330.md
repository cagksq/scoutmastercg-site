A Scoutmaster is not…
… the boss of a company and youth leadership are not employees we discipline or hire and fire.
A Scoutmaster is not a commanding officer and Scouts are not soldiers who can be disciplined for “conduct unbecoming” a Scout, so let's get that foolish idea out of our heads as well.
In this podcast I'll share what a Scoutmaster is, and the special role they have in young people's lives.
I'll also answer email questions about service hours, and how Scouts should address Scouters.
