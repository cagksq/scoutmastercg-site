We ended up drawing a kind of dividing line …
… between adult and Scout responsibilities, and it has a lot to do with Scouts and money.
In this podcast, in answer to a listener's question, I'll discuss how we decided who would be responsible for Scouts and money. I do think there's
some
return in having Scouts be responsible for administrative paperwork (permission slips, collecting money, etc.) but listen in and see if you think differently than I do about the issue.
Be sure to listen carefully to the thoughts one listener shared about the aspirations of Scouting and our country, the way he explained this to his son is really worth hearing.
