In a partnership with Discovery Channel UK the Scouts association published this list of 14 things for young people to do before they are 14:

1.

Navigate using a map

2.

Climb a tree

3. Make and fly a kite

4. Cook a meal

5.

Repair a bike

6. Camp outdoors with your friends

7.

Build a den (survival hut?)

8. Put up a tent

9. Ride a sledge (sled)

10. Use a telescope to look at the stars

11.

Light a fire safely

12.

Learn basic first aid 13. Go on a nature trail

14. Tie a reef knot What’s your list for fourteen things to do before you’re fourteen?
