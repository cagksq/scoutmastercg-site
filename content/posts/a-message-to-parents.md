If you Respect me, I will hear you. If you Listen to me, I will feel understood. If you Understand me, I will feel appreciated. If you Appreciate me, I will know your support. If you Support me as I try new things, I will become responsible. When I am Responsible, I will grow to be independent. In my Independence, I will respect you and love you all of my life.

Thank you. From Parent As Coach by Diana Haskins
