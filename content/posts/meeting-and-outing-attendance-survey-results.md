Here’s the results from the Outings and Meetings Attendance Survey. 48 different troops responded to the survey, most have between 20-40 Scouts: The vast majority meet weekly: Most see 50-75% at unit meetings Most troops conduct monthly outings or events 25-50% of Scouts attend these outings or events Of troops that use permission slips the majority distribute them from one to four weeks before the outing or event. What attracts Scouts to meetings and outings? What keeps them away? We’ll take a look at those questions and more tomorrow.
