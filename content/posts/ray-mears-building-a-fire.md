Ray Mears is an expert woodsman from southern Great Britain. I remember watching him on a U.S. cable network a few years ago. They can still be found on UKTV but I can't find them in the U.S. Thankfully there are a number of clips online.

Mears is a serious student and teacher of bushcraft — not a sensationalist unlike another British import whose show is (unfortunately) much more popular on U.S. televisions. He has authored many books available at his website, along with a selection of gear and related items.
