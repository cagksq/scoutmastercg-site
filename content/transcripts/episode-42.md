<p>And that's two out of three who had been in just for a brief amount of time. What is hazing exactly? I mean, hazing can be a ritual or other activities that use harassment, abuse, or humiliation as a way of initiating a person into a group. That's one way hazing can be looked at.</p>

<p>All hazing is antithetical to the spirit of scouting. Well, how do we know if it's hazing? If an activity or a practice or a tradition is intended to humiliate or trick or abuse or harass, it's hazing. And you've got to put these things to a stop immediately.</p>
<p>Some of these things may even be a hallowed tradition in your scout troop. But it's got to be stopped. And it's got to be eliminated.
